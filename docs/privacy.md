
# Shop Math Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Shop Math does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It makes no network connections. The measurements and lumber lists you enter are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
